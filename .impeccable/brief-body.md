# Surface brief: Portfolio site (all pages, TR + EN)

Scope: index/anasayfa, about/hakkimda, projects/projeler, gallery/galeri, blog/blog-tr, contact/iletisim. Mode: Experience (blog articles read in Read mode inside the same world).

Audience: course jury grading a midterm; secondary peers and employers. Job: see who Yunus is, his work, photos, writing, and reach him. Constraints: static files opened from disk (file://) as well as a host, so no external SVG <use>, no fetch; mailto form; Google Maps iframe.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Yunus's own pattern plate book: every page a numbered Plate (Levha), every project, photo and article a numbered unit with a ruled caption at its foot. Refuses the category's split hero + three equal cards on white.

OWN-WORLD: Slate #3C4246 and oatmeal #E8E4DA plate stock, stone #6B6F72 and fog #A7AAA7 for secondary ink, sand #D6CDBA for recessed fields, one madder red #B02E2E (kökboya, the kilim root dye) reserved for the active/selected unit and primary action. Fine 1px slate keylines frame every unit; Anatolian/Seljuk geometric ornament units (diamond, eight-point star, hooked cross-stitch rhombus) drawn as hard-edged SVG; a repeating border band. Type: Tektur constructed display caps with squared terminals; Commissioner body; Martian Mono tracked caps for plate numbers and captions.

STORY: The visitor reads a name and a plate, sees the work at once, understands he codes and designs, browses plates by number, opens any project/photo/article, and writes or calls him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left slate-edged vertical rail with monogram, numbered plate nav (01–06) and TR/EN switch; main field (no kicker): huge Tektur name (three lines: YUNUS / EMRE / ERSOY, since two lines exceed the 6rem display cap at this column width), one-line role, madder primary action "See the work" + secondary "Write to me"; right half: the portrait set inside a keyline frame on a carved-stone slate panel with a ruled caption. Below the fold a full-width ornament band, then a four-cell plate row (About summary, three featured projects as units).

FORM: Breton ornament program (catalog challenger design-canon-breton-ornament-program, adopted by the user over the roll's assigned Registrar's File), fused with Anatolian kilim/Seljuk stone ornament. seed:1581c17e8a0fbd0d. Signature interaction: selecting a unit (project, photo, article tag) slides a red ornament unit into the border and locks it as the active marker; the gallery lightbox opens the photo as a full plate with its caption.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
