Featured Projects

Some of my current work developed in the fields of software and design.

Image Colorization

This project is a two-stage adventure: First, contour and form with charcoal. Then, I added color layers one by one with digital brushes, as if working on physical paper. By enriching the plays of light and shadow with colors, I ensured the image gained depth.

Snake Game

Classics never die! This snake game was one of the first games I made when I started my coding adventure. It's here for a bit of nostalgia and to showcase my skills. Controls are simple: guide the snake with arrow keys, don't hit your tail, and collect the apples!

Product Promotion

This product promotion poster was designed from scratch using Adobe Photoshop. To reflect the brand's modern and dynamic identity; layer styles, advanced masking techniques, and highlights created with custom brushes were used. Considering color psychology in the selected palette, the aim was to make the product stand out and arouse desire in the target audience.
